# Man Overboard theory audit

Status: release blocked pending recorded qualified seamanship and medical approval.

Reviewed 2026-08-12 against the RYA man-overboard safety advisory, MCA MGN 570 emergency-drill/recovery guidance, current HM Coastguard GMDSS distress procedure guidance, and Resuscitation Council UK 2025 first-aid guidance for drowning and hypothermia.

The theory now uses a vessel-dependent decision model rather than universal manoeuvre distances or approach rules. It covers immediate alarm/flotation/mark/lookout actions; concurrent helm, communication and recovery roles; DSC plus Channel 16 MAYDAY; preparation, controlled approach and abort; propeller exclusion; rated securing/lifting; airway-aware near-horizontal cold-casualty recovery; aftercare and rescue escalation; and prevention, tethered and single-handed limitations.
