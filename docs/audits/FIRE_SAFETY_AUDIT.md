# Fire safety content audit

Last technical review: 2026-08-11

Scope: UK/EN equipment/classification guidance plus the escape-first onboard response, engine-space sequence, prevention and applied decision checks in the Fire Safety lesson.

## Findings addressed

- Uses BS EN 2 classes A, B, C, D and F. Energised electrical equipment is identified as a hazard, not “Class E”.
- Cooking oil/fat is Class F. Wet chemical must carry a Class F rating; blankets are separate equipment and limited to a small pan that can be approached and completely covered safely.
- Medium names no longer imply universal suitability. Learners are told to check the actual cylinder rating, approval and manufacturer instructions.
- Scenario answers allow condition-dependent alternatives and state prerequisites and limitations. CO2 is not labelled “ideal” for engine rooms; foam is not absolute for liquid fuel; powder cloud, breathing, visibility, residue, cooling and re-ignition limitations are explicit.
- Response starts with alarm, crew accounting/muster and escape, then early distress communication. Offshore, alongside/inland and immediate-evacuation decisions are distinguished.
- Isolation and firefighting require safe access, a protected escape route, vessel/manufacturer instructions and approved equipment. Unprotected smoke entry is prohibited.
- The closed engine-space sequence covers evacuation, remote shutdowns, maintaining enclosure, approved-system discharge, re-ignition monitoring and no reopening without the stated hold time and competent advice.
- Prevention covers detection, servicing/approval, placement, drills, escape routes, heating, batteries/charging, shore power and emergency equipment. Applied checks assess response decisions, not extinguisher matching.

## Sources checked

- RYA safety guidance on fire prevention and firefighting aboard recreational craft.
- RYA guidance on Mayday procedure and preparing for emergencies at sea.
- Maritime and Coastguard Agency guidance, including MGN 681 (M), for marine fire hazards and competent equipment selection, and MGN 280 small-vessel fire-appliance/escape provisions.
- MCA/GOV.UK guidance on distress alerts and use of VHF DSC.
- Boat Safety Scheme fire-fighting, escape, LPG, electrical and fuel-system requirements for inland craft.
- UK Government fire-safety guidance and BS EN 2 classification conventions.
- The marked rating, approvals and manufacturer instructions for the particular equipment remain authoritative.

## Release gate

The source review above is an editorial technical review, not competent-person approval. The route fails closed: the lesson, drill and completion controls are withheld. Before release, a competent marine fire-safety professional must review the rendered lesson and every equipment and response scenario, then record their name, qualification, approval date and non-empty source evidence. `FIRE_SAFETY_RELEASE_REVIEW.reviewed` must remain `false` and those approval fields null/empty until that evidence exists.
