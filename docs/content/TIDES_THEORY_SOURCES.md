# Understanding Tides sources and visual provenance

## Teaching sources

The lesson's physical model and terminology are based on the authoritative
sources linked from the learner-facing page:

- NOAA/NESDIS, *What Causes Tides?*
- NOAA National Ocean Service, *What are spring and neap tides?*
- UK Maritime and Coastguard Agency, *Navigation and Radar Examination
  Syllabus*

These sources inform the teaching text; no source artwork or chart data is
reproduced.

## Amphidromic schematic provenance and licence

The inline SVG `NorthSeaAmphidromicDiagram` in
`src/pages/TidalTheory.tsx` was created directly in this repository by the
Cleanship Day Skipper Trainer project for issue #244 on 9 August 2026. It is
an original explanatory schematic, not a transcription of a hydrographic
chart, and uses no external geographic, co-tidal, co-range, or port-prediction
dataset.
