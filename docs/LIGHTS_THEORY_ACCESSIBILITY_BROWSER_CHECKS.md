# Lights theory browser accessibility checks

The component suite protects names, landmarks, keyboard-operable evidence controls, non-disruptive live announcements, and structured equivalents for light colour, vertical order, observer aspect/sector arcs, and day-shape combinations.

Before release, verify `/rules/lights/theory` in Chromium with no horizontal document overflow at 320 px and 375 px, then at 200% and 400% browser zoom. Check all four tabs, the Rule 22 table's contained scroll region, every light-plan card, the dredging three-part shape plan, and completion evidence.

Keyboard-only checks: the Back control has a meaningful name; tabs follow the expected arrow-key pattern and retain visible focus; every checkbox, radio and button has visible focus; recording evidence, a failed save, and successful/queued completion are announced through the polite status region without moving focus. Confirm headings and the banner/main landmarks provide a useful reading order.

With Windows High Contrast or Chromium forced-colours emulation enabled, verify light marks retain an outline while their adjacent colour/position labels remain readable, and balls, diamonds, cylinders and cones remain distinguishable. Visual colour is supplementary: the structured captions and ordered lists must independently state colour, light type, vertical order, fore/aft placement, observer aspect and shape arrangement.
